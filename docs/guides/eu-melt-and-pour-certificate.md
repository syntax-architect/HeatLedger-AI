# EU Melt and Pour Certificate: What to Put on an MTC

> **Regulatory Reference**: Regulation (EU) 2026/1384 & Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1963  
> **Last Reviewed**: 21 November 2026  
> **Status**: Official Regulatory Guidance for Steel Exporters

---

## 1. Statutory Background

Starting **1 October 2026**, all importers of covered iron and steel goods into the European Union must evidence the **Country of Melt and Pour** directly upon customs clearance.

Under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/1963, this evidence must be provided via a standard **Mill Test Certificate (MTC)** complying with EN 10204 3.1 or ISO 10474 standards. Other supporting commercial documents may stand alone only during the transitional window ending **30 September 2027**; thereafter, non-MTC documents may only supplement an authentic mill certificate.

Official source: [EUR-Lex Regulation (EU) 2026/1384](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202601384).

---

## 2. Mandatory Elements on the Certificate

To clear EU border inspection without statutory delays, every upstream mill certificate must distinctly display:

1. **Originating Melt Country**: The exact sovereign state where raw liquid steel was produced in an Electric Arc Furnace (EAF) or Basic Oxygen Furnace (BOF).
2. **Furnace Heat / Cast Number**: The immutable metallurgical batch number tied to the liquid ladle melt.
3. **Primary Steelmaker Identification**: Registered legal name, melt shop facility address, and official quality inspection seal.
4. **Product Specification & Grade**: Exact steel grade (e.g., S275JR, S355J2, 316L) with ladle chemical analysis (C, Si, Mn, P, S, Cr, Ni, Mo).
5. **Physical Dimensions & Quantity**: Nominal thickness, width/diameter, bundle/coil count, and gross/net mass in metric tonnes.

---

## 3. Common Customs Non-Conformances

- **Intermediate Processor Country Stated**: Stating the country of cold-rolling or tube drawing rather than the origin of molten steel.
- **Missing Ladle Heat Cross-Reference**: Commercial invoices quoting heat numbers that do not match the attached mill test certificate.
- **Unsealed Electronic Certificates**: Submitting unverified photocopies lacking cryptographic tamper-evidence or inspector verification.
