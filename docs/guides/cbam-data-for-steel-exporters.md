# CBAM Data for Steel Exporters: What the Exporter Supplies

> **Regulatory Reference**: Regulation (EU) 2023/956 & CBAM Communication Guidance  
> **Last Reviewed**: 21 November 2026  
> **Status**: Compliance Protocol for Non-EU Fabricators and Mills

---

## 1. Who Pays and Who Provides Data?

Under the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM):
- **The Importer Pays**: Financial liability for purchasing CBAM certificates rests entirely with the EU-authorised declarant / importer.
- **The Exporter Supplies Data**: Non-EU steel fabricators and exporters must provide primary installations data (direct and indirect specific embedded emissions) so importers avoid statutory default markups.

The definitive regime commenced on **1 January 2026**. The first formal declaration covering 2026 imports is statutory due by **30 September 2027**.

Official source: [European Commission CBAM Official Portal](https://taxation-customs.ec.europa.eu/carbon-border-adjustment-mechanism_en).

---

## 2. What Specific Data Must You Supply?

For every exported shipment of covered steel goods, exporters must supply a structured CBAM Declaration Package comprising:

1. **Installation Identification**: Facility coordinates, country of manufacture, and technology route (Blast Furnace-BOF vs. Scrap EAF).
2. **Specific Direct Emissions ($SEE_{direct}$)**: Expressed in metric tonnes of $\text{CO}_2\text{e}$ per metric tonne of steel produced ($\text{tCO}_2\text{e}/\text{t}$).
3. **Specific Indirect Emissions ($SEE_{indirect}$)**: Grid electricity factor and power consumption per tonne of finished output.
4. **Precursor Goods Embedded Carbon**: Tonnage and emissions profiles of consumed upstream raw inputs (sponge iron/DRI, billets, pig iron).
5. **Effective Carbon Price Paid**: Verified rebates or national taxes already levied in the host country (e.g., Indian Clean Environment Cess).

---

## 3. Why Default Values Cost More

When an exporter fails to supply primary data, EU customs applies the standard EU default value with an escalating punitive penalty markup:
- **2026**: Default value + **10% markup**
- **2027**: Default value + **20% markup**
- **2028 onwards**: Default value + **30% markup**

Supplying verified actual emissions saves EU buyers between **€18 to €45 per tonne** compared to punitive default baselines.
