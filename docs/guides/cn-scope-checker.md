# CN Scope Checker: Melt-and-Pour vs. CBAM Tariff Classification

> **Regulatory Reference**: Regulation (EU) 2026/1384 Annex I & Regulation (EU) 2023/956 Annex I  
> **Last Reviewed**: 21 November 2026  
> **Status**: Border Classification Matrix

---

## 1. The Scope Distinction Every Exporter Must Know

A common and costly misunderstanding in steel trade is conflating **Melt-and-Pour Scope** with general **CBAM Scope**. They are governed by distinct legal annexes:

| Tariff Headings | Product Categories | Melt-and-Pour Evidence Required? | CBAM Emissions Reporting Required? |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :---: |
| **CN 7208 to 7229** | Flat-rolled steel, bars, wire rod, structural angles | **YES (Mandatory)** | **YES** |
| **CN 7301 to 7306** | Sheet piling, railway track, welded pipes, seamless tubes | **YES (Mandatory)** | **YES** |
| **CN 7307** | Tube and pipe fittings (flanges, elbows, couplings) | **NO (Exempt until review)** | **YES (Mandatory)** |
| **CN 7318** | Fasteners, screws, bolts, nuts, coach screws, rivets | **NO (Exempt until review)** | **YES (Mandatory)** |
| **CN 7326** | Other forged, stamped or fabricated articles of iron/steel | **NO** | **YES** |

Official source: [Regulation (EU) 2026/1384 Annex I](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202601384).

---

## 2. Key Takeaways for Fasteners & Fittings (CN 7307 & 7318)

- **Commission Review Due 30 June 2027**: Fasteners (CN 7318) and fittings (CN 7307) are **NOT** currently within the melt-and-pour country verification mandate. Customs authorities cannot block CN 7318 cargo for lacking ladle heat numbers.
- **Still Subject to CBAM**: Although exempt from melt-and-pour rules, they **ARE** subject to CBAM emissions reporting.

---

## 3. United Kingdom (UK) Timeline

- **1 April 2027 – 30 June 2027**: Voluntary reporting window for UK melt-and-pour evidence.
- **1 July 2027**: Mandatory melt-and-pour enforcement commences across UK customs ports.
- **Exemption Threshold**: Consignments valued under **£2,000 GBP** are fully exempt from UK melt-and-pour reporting.

Official source: [GOV.UK Melt-and-Pour Reporting Guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/reporting-the-country-of-melt-and-pour-for-certain-steel-imports).
