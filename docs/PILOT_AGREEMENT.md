## 2. Customer Data Handling & Confidentiality One-Pager

### Protocol Summary (5 Guarantees)

1. **Exclusive Customer Ownership**:
   - All customer documents, mill test certificates, commercial invoices, chemical analyses, and buyer identities remain the exclusive, perpetual property of Customer.

2. **Byte-Identical Original Preservation (EN 10204 Clause 6)**:
   - Original supplier MTC files uploaded by Customer are hashed with SHA-256 and stored untouched and unmodified. HeatLedger never alters original mill documents.

3. **Zero AI Model Training on Private Data**:
   - Customer certificates, proprietary chemistry data, and dispatch records are strictly segregated and are **never used to train public machine learning or foundation models**.

4. **Cryptographic Tenant Isolation (Row-Level Security)**:
   - Data is stored in secure Indian / EU cloud infrastructure with PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS) enforcing complete cryptographic isolation between organizations.

5. **Data Portability & Right of Deletion**:
   - Customer may download a complete ZIP archive of all certificates, ledger hashes, and dossiers at any time. Customer may request complete cryptographic purging of all plant data with thirty (30) days statutory written notice.
