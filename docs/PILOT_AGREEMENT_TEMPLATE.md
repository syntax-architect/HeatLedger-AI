**PILOT EVALUATION & COMPLIANCE DATA HANDLING AGREEMENT**

2. **[PARTICIPATING EXPORTER NAME]** ("Pilot Customer", "Plant"), located at [FACTORY ADDRESS, e.g., Howrah / Liluah / Ludhiana], India.

### 1. Purpose of the Pilot
The Customer desires to evaluate the HeatLedger compliance operating system for shop-floor certificate ingestion, EN 10204:2004 Clause 6 byte-identical original annexing, and European Union (EU) CBAM Regulation 2023/1773 & EU 2026/1963 Country of Melt and Pour compliance readiness.

### 2. Pilot Term & Scope of Service
* **Duration:** Thirty (30) days from deployment or completion of Customer's first three (3) export shipment dossiers, whichever occurs first.
* **Scope:** Free 1-shipment compliance audit, full platform access for up to five (5) designated plant personnel (Plant Owner, QA Inspector, Dispatch Officer, Compliance Auditor), and dedicated onboarding support.

### 3. Customer Data Protection & Plant Data Sovereignty
* **Byte-Identical Preservation:** Customer's original Mill Test Certificates (MTCs) and test reports are ingested without digital alteration, overprinting, or destructive re-compression.
* **Zero Data Retention Inference:** Optical character recognition (OCR) and document extraction utilize dedicated Google Gemini Zero Data Retention Enterprise APIs. Customer data is strictly excluded from third-party model training datasets.
* **Right of Revocation & Erasure:** In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), Customer maintains sole sovereignty over its documents. Customer may export or request complete cryptographic erasure of all stored certificates and ledger entries upon thirty (30) days' written notice.

### 4. Regulatory Disclaimer & Allocation of Responsibilities
* HeatLedger AI is a specialized software compliance preparation tool. **HeatLedger is not a law firm, not an accredited verification body (AVB), and not an authorised inspection representative.**
* All final metallurgical release determinations, EN 10204 3.1 digital signatures, and CBAM declaration filings remain the sole statutory responsibility of the Customer's designated Qualified QA Inspector.

### 5. Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure
All proprietary commercial invoices, heat numbers, mill purchase prices, and client destination port details shall be treated as Confidential Information and shall not be disclosed to any third party without express prior consent.
