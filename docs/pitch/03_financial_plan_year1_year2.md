## 3. Key Operational Assumptions & Sensitivity
1. **Pilot-to-Paid Conversion:** Assumes 60% conversion because the full ₹25,000 fee is credited toward the first 3 months of subscription, eliminating financial hesitation.
2. **Channel Economics:** 20% commission paid to CAs, export consultants, and freight forwarders for the first 12 months. This keeps customer acquisition cost below 2 months of subscription value.
3. **Regulatory Catalyst:** 1 Oct 2026 enforcement creates a hard compliance gate; export plants cannot tender without verified melt-and-pour documentation.
