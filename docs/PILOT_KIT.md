## Page 3: Onboarding Call Script (8 Questions)

1. **Shipment Volume**: *"How many export containers or consignments do you dispatch to the EU, UK, or US per month?"*
2. **Current CN / HS Codes**: *"Which CN codes do you primarily export (e.g., 7208–7306 carbon/alloy steel, or downstream 7307 fittings / 7318 fasteners)?"*
3. **MTC Sources**: *"Where do your mill test certificates come from — direct primary producers (Tata, SAIL, JSW), re-rollers, or stockists?"*
4. **Country of Melt and Pour**: *"Do your current supplier MTCs explicitly state 'Country of Melt and Pour', or only 'Country of Origin'?"*
5. **Traceability Process**: *"How does your shop floor currently match heat numbers on billets/bars to the final commercial invoice?"*
6. **Buyer Pushback**: *"Have any European buyers or customs brokers recently asked for CBAM emissions data or heat-traceable MTCs?"*
7. **Document Sign-Off**: *"Who at your plant is authorized to review and sign off on outgoing inspection certificates?"*
8. **ERP & Accounting**: *"Do you use Tally, SAP, or manual Excel spreadsheets for inventory and dispatch records?"*
